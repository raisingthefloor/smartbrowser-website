Challenge awards

Cash awards for working proofs of concept that advance key parts of the smart browser, open source so everyone can build on them. The awards are funded; the first challenges and their official rules are being finalized.

How the awards work

  • Each challenge names a capability or milestone, what a successful demonstration must do, and how it will be judged.
  • Entries must be released under an open-source license (Apache 2.0 or compatible) so that anyone, including browser vendors, can use them.
  • Judging weighs whether it works for people with disabilities, whether it runs on ordinary hardware, how little model work it needs, and how clearly it shows its changes.
  • Awards are given when a challenge's bar is met, not on a deadline. Several entries can meet the same bar; the rules for each challenge say how awards are shared.
  • Official rules, eligibility, and award amounts will be published here for each challenge before it opens.

Challenges under consideration

These are candidates, listed so that people can start thinking and tell us what is missing. None is open yet.

  • Fit a real front page

    Apply a person's settings to a large, live news front page without breaking its layout or scripts, within a time budget, on a mid-range laptop. Addresses open question 1.

  • Smallest model that labels controls

    Infer accurate names for unlabeled icon buttons and links on a test set of real pages using the smallest possible on-device model. Addresses open question 12.

  • Injection-resistant rewriting

    Demonstrate a fit pipeline in which page content can never direct the browser to change a form's action, a link's destination, or anything on an authentication page. Addresses open questions 3 and 4.

  • Reading level you can slide

    Rewrite a page's prose to a reading level the person sets, continuously, with the original one tap away and no change of meaning, on device.

  • Fit that survives live updates

    Keep a page fitted while it re-renders itself through live regions and scripts. Addresses open question 2.

  • Judged by the people it is for

    An evaluation method and results showing people with disabilities comparing fitted pages with what they have today, across several kinds of disability. Addresses open question 13.

Have a challenge to propose, or want to be told when the first ones open? Follow the project or email us.

Smart Browser is an open collaboration to prove that a browser can fit any web page to the person using it. Everyone who joins the effort is part of it. This site is sponsored by Raising the Floor.
