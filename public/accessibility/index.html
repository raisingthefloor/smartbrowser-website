Accessibility statement

This site is about fitting pages to people, so it had better be usable by everyone. Here is what we aim for, what we know falls short, and how to tell us when something does not work.

Our target

Every page on smartbrowser.site, except the demonstration pages described below, is intended to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at Level AA, and to be fully usable with a screen reader, with keyboard alone, with magnification and large text, with user style sheets, in high contrast, and with motion reduced.

What we did

  • Real headings, landmarks, and a skip link on every page; link text that makes sense on its own.
  • Text and background colors checked for contrast in both light and dark themes; no information carried by color alone.
  • Everything works without JavaScript. The interactive demonstration on the home page is decorative for assistive technology; the changes it illustrates are written out as text beside it.
  • No motion starts on its own except a brief blink inside the home-page demonstration, which stops within five seconds and is disabled entirely when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • A Display control on every page lets you choose larger text, more spacing, and a light or dark theme, in the spirit of the project. Your browser's own zoom and text-size settings also work.
  • Fonts are your system's own, so your installed accessibility fonts apply, and no third-party font service is contacted.

Deliberately inaccessible pages

Pages under /demo/ are intentionally inaccessible. They exist so that a smart browser has something to fix. Each one carries a warning at the top and a link back here. They are excluded from the conformance target above.

Tell us

If anything on this site does not work for you, we want to know and we will fix it. Email gregg@raisingthefloor.org or open an issue on GitHub. Please say which page and, if you can, what you were using.

This statement was written in November 2026 and will be updated as the site changes.
