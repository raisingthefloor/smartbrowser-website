Demonstration pages

These pages are deliberately inaccessible. They are test material for smart browsers and are excluded from this site's accessibility commitment. If you rely on assistive technology, expect missing labels, unreadable contrast, and worse. Everything a demo page says is also available in accessible form on the page that links to it.

Each demo page is a plausible, fictional web page with specific, documented accessibility failures, so that a smart browser's fit can be seen and measured.

  • Transit service notice

    The sample page from the home-page demonstration, as a real page. Failures: text too small and too low in contrast, justified text, unlabeled icon buttons, a blinking alert, an image with no alternative text, a “Click here” link, an idiom and unexpanded abbreviations, a form field with no label, and a color-only error message. Reading level: college. List of failures and the WCAG criteria involved.

More demo pages will be added as the proof of concept's test set grows. To contribute one, open an issue on this site's repository with the page and the list of failures it is meant to contain.

Smart Browser is an open collaboration to prove that a browser can fit any web page to the person using it. Everyone who joins the effort is part of it. This site is sponsored by Raising the Floor.

The proof of concept was funded in part by the National Science Foundation (Grant 2312370) and the National Institute on Disability, Independent Living, and Rehabilitation Research, Administration for Community Living, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (Grant 90REGE008).
